# TASK 6 – FUNCTIONS IN PYTHON

## Objective
To divide the milk payment calculator into reusable functions.

## Functions Used

1. **validate_entry()** – Checks whether the farmer details satisfy the validation rules.
2. **calculate_rate()** – Calculates the milk rate using fat and SNF adjustments.
3. **calculate_payment()** – Multiplies the milk quantity by the calculated rate.

## Advantages of Functions
- Reduce repeated code.
- Improve readability.
- Make testing and debugging easier.
- Allow individual operations to be reused.
- Make the program easier to maintain.

## Sample Result
For farmer F001 supplying 10 litres with 3.7% fat and 8.6% SNF:
- Rate per litre = ₹31.10
- Total payment = ₹311.00

## Conclusion
Functions divide the program into smaller units, making the milk payment calculator easier to understand, test, and maintain.
