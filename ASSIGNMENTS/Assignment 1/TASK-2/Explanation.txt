# TASK 2 – MILK PAYMENT CALCULATION

## Objective
To calculate the payment for milk supplied by a farmer based on milk quantity, fat percentage, and SNF percentage.

## Formula
Fat adjustment = ((Fat percentage − 3.5) / 0.1) × ₹0.40

SNF adjustment = ((SNF percentage − 8.5) / 0.1) × ₹0.30

Rate per litre = ₹30.00 + Fat adjustment + SNF adjustment

Total payment = Milk quantity × Rate per litre

## Sample Input
Farmer ID: F001  
Milk quantity: 10 litres  
Fat percentage: 3.7%  
SNF percentage: 8.6%

## Expected Calculation
Fat adjustment = ₹0.80  
SNF adjustment = ₹0.30  
Rate per litre = ₹31.10  
Total payment = ₹311.00

## Conclusion
The program calculates the milk rate using fat and SNF adjustments and multiplies the resulting rate by the quantity of milk supplied.
